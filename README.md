# SVPP Autonomous — Department of Artificial Intelligence Portal

Official academic portal and real-time attendance & examination system for **Sri Venkatesa Perumal College of Engineering & Technology (SVPP Autonomous)**, Puttur.

---

## 🌟 Key Modules & Features

- **Department Overview & AI Infrastructure Showcase**:
  - Advanced AI, Machine Learning & Robotics Research Center
  - NVIDIA GPU Nodes, Edge AI Systems (Jetson Orin), and 1 Gbps backbone
- **Real-Time Attendance Portal (Google Sheets Model)**:
  - 3-row headers with period counts, lecture dates (`17/6`, `18/6`...), and period numbers
  - Color-coded presence (`1`) and red absents (`A`)
  - Course-specific databases (NLP, OS, Computer Vision, EDA, Operating Systems, Labs)
  - Role-based separation: view-only for students, live registers & gazette authorization for Faculty and HOD
- **III Year (V Sem) Class Roster**:
  - Class directory for 127 AI students across Section A and Section B
  - Instant 1-click ledger & exam lookup
- **Autonomous Syllabus (R26 Regulation)**:
  - Approved Board of Studies (BoS) curriculum copy and unit breakdowns
- **Semester Examination Results Portal**:
  - Official grade memos with SGPA/CGPA evaluation, credit tracking, and printable format
- **Mid Marks & Internals Assessment**:
  - Descriptive test, quiz, assignment, and scaled internal scores
